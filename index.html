Research Overview

Metabolism-aware antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) addresses a critical blind spot in clinical decision support: many bacterial metabolic processes influence antibiotic efficacy, yet traditional AST methods often ignore metabolic activity. By integrating metabolic modeling with susceptibility testing, this approach aims to predict antibiotic effectiveness more accurately, particularly for bacteria with active metabolism or those entering stationary phase. Addressing this metabolic blind spot could improve treatment outcomes, reduce antibiotic overuse, and combat the rise of resistant strains.
